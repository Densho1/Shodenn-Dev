Account & Data Deletion Request

App: Kanje | Developer: SHODENN

If you have created an account in Kanje using Google Sign-In and wish to request the deletion of your account and associated data, please follow the options below.

How to Delete Your Account and Associated Data

  1. Automatic Local Data Removal: Uninstalling the Kanje application from your Android device immediately deletes all local app data, study history, and SRS progress stored on your device.
  2. Google Drive Cloud Backup Deletion:
    • You can directly delete your cloud backup file (kanje_backup.json) by going to your Google Drive on the web > Settings (gear icon) > Manage Apps > Kanje > Options > Delete hidden app data.
    • Alternatively, send a request to us to handle this process (see below).
  3. Revoke App Access: You can revoke Kanje's permissions from your Google Account at any time by visiting Google Account Third-Party Access Settings.

Submit Deletion Request via Email

If you prefer us to assist you in deleting your account and backup data from our records, send an email to:

Email: support@shodenn.com
Subject: Kanje Account Deletion Request
Include: The Google email address used when signing into Kanje.

Data Handling & Retention Policy

  • Data Deleted: Your Google User Account ID, email address, SRS study progress, lesson history, and Google Drive cloud backups.
  • Data Retained: Anonymous Google Play purchase/transaction records are retained as required by financial law and managed directly by Google Play.
  • Retention Period: Manual email deletion requests are fulfilled within 14 business days.
