Privacy Policy for Kanje

Effective Date: May 2024 (or Today's Date)

This Privacy Policy applies to the Kanje mobile application (the "App"), developed and operated by Shodenn ("we", "us", or "our"). This document informs users of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information for anyone using the App.

1. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

a. User Authentication (Google Sign-In)

If you choose to log in using Google Sign-In or Google Identity Services, we collect basic account information provided by Google, such as your email address and basic profile information. This information is used solely to authenticate your account, secure your profile, and sync your study progress.

b. In-App Purchases & Billing

The App uses Google Play Billing to process purchases for premium content or level unlocks (such as JLPT level extensions). All payment transactions are processed directly and securely by Google. We do not collect, store, or have access to your financial or payment details (such as credit card information).

c. Local Storage & App Usage

Your learning progress, quiz scores, dictionary settings, and preferences are saved locally on your device (using Android Room database and DataStore preferences). This data remains on your device and is used solely to operate the App's learning functionality.

2. Third-Party Services

The App integrates with third-party service providers that may collect information according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Billing: Handles purchase processing securely.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share information with third-party services as necessary to facilitate user authentication and Google Play payment processing.

4. Data Retention and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any data associated with your account. Since primary app data is stored locally on your device, you can clear this data at any time by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the App.

5. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
